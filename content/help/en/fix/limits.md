## Input lengths and counts {#length}

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| Meeting name | 200 characters |
| Meeting topic | 200 characters |
| Meeting background | 1,000 characters |
| Meeting attendees | 200 accounts and 200 guest participants |
| Project name | 100 characters |
| Guest participant name | 100 characters |
| Member name shown in this workspace | 30 characters |
| One glossary term | 40 characters |
| Glossary | 200 terms per workspace |
| Pasting terms | 100 at a time |
| Projects and meeting notes added as scope when asking the agent | 20 in total |
| Help & feedback request | 1,200 characters |
| Message in a meeting notes email | 1,000 characters |

## Rate limits {#rates}

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| Invites | 30 per person per day, 3 to the same address |
| Invites one address can receive | 10 per day in total |
| Help & feedback requests | 5 per person per hour |
| Meeting notes emails | 30 per hour, 10 recipients at a time |
| PDF attached to a meeting notes email | 10 MB or less |
| Meeting result emails sent by My agent | 30 per day |

## Durations and time {#time}

| What | Duration |
|---|---|
| Sign-in | Expires after 30 days without use |
| Invite link | 1 day after it's sent |
| Outside AI app connection | Expires after 90 days without use |
| Meeting notes PDF sent by email | Kept for 30 days |

- Meeting analysis: If it doesn't finish within 12 minutes, it shows as failed. Click **[Reanalyze]** to request it again.
- Lost connection to the recording device: After about 1 minute, you can continue recording from somewhere else.

## Rules to know {#rules}

There are only two roles: admin and member.

- Only admins can: Change workspace settings, manage integrations and MCP, invite and remove members, rename, link, and delete guest participants, and delete projects.
- Any member can: View notes, record, end meetings, confirm reviews, and edit tasks.

> [!Can't be undone]
> You can't record more in a meeting that has ended, and ==you can't undo a confirmed review.== You can't delete a meeting that is recording, has finished analysis, or has been confirmed.
