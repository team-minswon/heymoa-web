## What shows up {#what}

The **[Timeline]** tab sorts what was said into decisions, tasks, open questions, and notes each time someone finishes speaking. Each item comes with the words it was based on. Click the timestamp to jump to that line in the transcript.

Proposals nobody accepted, objections, simple agreement, and remarks that only keep the meeting moving don't become items. So not everything said in the meeting shows up.

> [!Note]
> While recording, HeyMoa doesn't know who is speaking, so items have no assignee. ==You set assignees and due dates in the summary review after the meeting ends.==

## Items that get updated or reversed {#changes}

When someone says the same thing again, HeyMoa updates the existing item instead of adding a new one. A new item has no label. An updated item shows one of these.

- **[Expanded]**: More was added to the same item.
- **[Corrected]**: Something said earlier was corrected.
- **[Reversed]**: Someone reversed the decision.
- **[Awaiting an answer]**: An open question hasn't been answered yet.
- **[Answered]**: An open question got an answer.

An item becomes **[Reversed]** only when someone actually reverses the decision. An objection alone doesn't reverse it.

## How to read the timeline {#view}

Filter by **[All]**, **[Decisions]**, **[Tasks]**, **[Open questions]**, or **[Reference]**. During recording the list follows the newest item. For an ended meeting it starts from the top.

If you're on the **[Transcript]** tab while recording, a tip bubble appears for 60 seconds when the first timeline item shows up. Click it to go to the **[Timeline]** tab.

The copy icon copies the whole timeline, whatever filter you picked. If a message says some changes were missed and the timeline is catching up, click **[Resync]**.

When you end the meeting, the summary is built from this timeline. The next step is covered in [Ending a meeting and analysis](help:meeting-end).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Does everything said in the meeting show up?
No. Proposals nobody accepted, objections, simple agreement, and remarks that only keep the meeting moving don't become items.

### Can I set an assignee on the timeline?
No. While recording, HeyMoa doesn't know who is speaking, so items have no assignee. You set assignees and due dates in the summary review after the meeting ends.

### Does an objection reverse a decision?
No. A decision is marked reversed only when someone actually reverses it. An objection alone doesn't reverse it.
