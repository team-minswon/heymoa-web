## What's happening {#problem}

You clicked record in the app, but recording doesn't start. Or it started, but the other person's voice isn't in the transcript.

## Where to look first {#where}

Check the app's intro window (it shows permission status), the window that opens from the menu bar icon (the popover), and any error message on the meeting screen.

## Try these steps {#steps}

1. **Read the error message in the app**
   If it mentions microphone or system audio permission, it's a permissions problem.
2. **If the message only says “Couldn't start recording”, check everything below**
   The cause hasn't been narrowed down. Check both your connection and your permissions.
3. **On a Mac, allow the permissions in System Settings**
   In **[Privacy & Security]**, turn on microphone and system audio recording for HeyMoa.
4. **On Windows, allow access on the microphone privacy screen**
   It's the microphone privacy screen in Settings.
5. **Go back to the app and click [Check again ↻]**
   This step applies only while the intro window is open. Skip it if you've already finished the intro.
6. **Check your internet connection**
   If you're offline, reconnect first.
7. **Start recording again**
   Click record again after changing the settings.
8. **If it still fails, quit the app completely and open it again**
   Then try starting the recording.
9. **If the other person's voice is missing, check that you can hear it on that computer**
   ==Sound you can't hear isn't recorded.==
10. **If you see “Sound capture stopped”, click [Resume]**
    ==Recording has paused.== Pick it back up on the meeting screen.
11. **If you see “Couldn't sign in”, click the sign-in button again**
    Start signing in again from the beginning.
