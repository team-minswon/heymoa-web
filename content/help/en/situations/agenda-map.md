## What's happening {#problem}

You covered the same topic over several meetings and want to see at a glance how far it has come.

The Connection map draws how a project's meetings were linked through their agenda items on one screen. Meetings run across (in time order), and topics run down.

## Where to look first {#where}

Use the **[Notes]** **[Connection map]** tabs that appear when you click a project. You can also use the **[Connection map]** chip at the top of a meeting summary, or **[⋯]** → **[Connection map]** on the project row in the sidebar.

![A project's note list with the Notes and Connection map tabs above it](img:agenda-map/entry.webp "The [Notes] and [Connection map] tabs appear when you click a project.")

## How to read it {#read}

![A connection map with meeting dates across and topics down](img:agenda-map/overview.webp "Meetings run across and topics run down. A key sits below the map.")

- Dot: one agenda item.
- Capsule with a number: several agenda items on the same topic were covered in one meeting.
- Thin line: the topic was not covered in the meetings in between.
- Curve that splits: another line of agenda items continued from one item.
- **[+number]**: how many collapsed agenda items are not drawn on the screen. Click it to expand them.

Agenda titles are hidden at first. Click around as below.

## Click to explore {#steps}

1. **Hover over a dot**
   A card shows that agenda item's title and summary.
2. **Click a meeting date**
   The agenda titles for that meeting open up.
3. **Click a dot**
   The right panel shows the summary, decisions, and the items before and after it. **[View in meeting notes]** takes you to that meeting.
4. **Click [View this topic larger]**
   Shows just that topic as a large timeline. Press `kbd:Esc` or **[All topics]** to go back.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Some meetings are missing
Yes. Only meetings with organized agenda items are drawn. If no items are linked yet, the map is empty and shows a short note.

### A link looks wrong
The links are found by AI, so they can be wrong. The map is read-only. Edit and confirm in the meeting notes.
