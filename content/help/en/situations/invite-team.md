## What's happening {#problem}

You want teammates to see and record meetings and tasks in the same workspace (the space your team shares).

## Where to look first {#where}

Go to **[Settings]** → **[Workspace]** → the **[Members]** tab. ==Only admins can invite.== If you're not an admin, you won't see **[Invite members]**.

## Try these steps {#steps}

1. **Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar**
   Open **[Workspace settings]** from the menu.
2. **Click [Members] on the left**
   The member list appears.
3. **Enter your teammate's email in the [Invite members] area**
   This is the email of the person you're inviting.
4. **Choose a role**
   Choose **[Member]** or **[Admin]**.
5. **Click [Invite]**
   The invite email is sent.
6. **Tell your teammate to accept**
   They can click the link in the invite email or click **[Accept]** in the notification bell.
7. **Make sure your teammate logs in with the right account**
   It must be the Google account for the invited email.
8. **If they don't accept within a day, click [Cancel]**
   It's under **[Pending invites]**.
9. **Invite the same email again**
   A new link is sent. ==Links are valid for 1 day (24 hours) only.==
