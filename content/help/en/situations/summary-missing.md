## What's happening {#problem}

The meeting is over, but there's no summary in the **[Summary]** tab. Or you see “Couldn't analyze the meeting”.

## Where to look first {#where}

In the note's **[Summary]** tab, look at the progress stages (**[Speaker separation]**, **[Analysis]**, **[Review]**, **[Confirm]**) and the message shown.

## Try these steps {#steps}

1. **Check that the meeting status is [Ended]**
   The status is shown at the top.
2. **If it's [Recording], click [Stop]**
   The recording hasn't finished yet.
3. **If it's [Paused], click [End meeting]**
   Analysis starts only after the meeting ends.
4. **Open the [Summary] tab**
   However, in the side view this tab appears only after the meeting ends.
5. **If you see “Analyzing the meeting”, wait**
   It can take a few minutes. You can go to another screen in the meantime.
6. **If you see “Waiting for analysis”, click [Request analysis]**
   Analysis starts.
7. **If you see “Couldn't analyze the meeting”, click [Reanalyze]**
   ==Your transcript and meeting record are still there.==
8. **Check whether the [Transcript] tab has content**
   ==If the transcript is nearly empty, there's nothing to analyze.==
9. **If it still doesn't work, send a request**
   Report it under **[Problem or error]** in **[Help & feedback]** in the sidebar.
