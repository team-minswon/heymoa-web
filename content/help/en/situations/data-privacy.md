## What's happening {#problem}

You want to know where your meeting content goes, how long it's kept, and what you can turn off or delete yourself.

## Where to look first {#where}

The legally binding explanation is the Privacy Policy. This page sums up only the parts of it people ask about most.

## How your data is handled {#steps}

- Recorded audio is sent in real time to a speech recognition provider and transcribed. It's also kept in encrypted storage in a data center region in Seoul (the Seoul region) for speaker separation and recovery.
- To write summaries and answers from My agent, HeyMoa sends that meeting's transcript and notes, attendee names, and your questions to an AI provider.
- When the agent uses web search, it sends only search terms made of everyday words.
- To have work done in Linear or GitHub, you must click **[Approve]** on the approval card.
- External AI apps can only read what you've given them access to. A connection expires after 90 days without use.
- ==Deleting a note also deletes that meeting's audio, transcript, and summary.== Only admins can delete notes, and while a meeting is being analyzed you can delete it once the analysis finishes.
- Meeting notes PDFs shared by email are kept for 30 days after sending. ==Invite links are valid for 1 day (24 hours) only.==
- If you turn off **[Send usage statistics]** in **[Account settings]**, this browser stops sending usage statistics.

For details, and for how to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
