## What's happening {#problem}

The transcript has an unnamed speaker like “Speaker B”. Or someone else's name is attached.

## Where to look first {#where}

In the **[Transcript]** tab of an ended meeting, look at the speaker name above each line. The **[Unassigned N]** button shows how many speakers are left.

## Try these steps {#steps}

::clip{name="speaker-assign" alt="Video of clicking an unnamed speaker in the transcript and choosing an attendee so the name is attached" caption="Clicking a speaker name and choosing an attendee."}

1. **Check that “Separating speakers” is gone**
   ==You can assign speakers once separation is finished.==
2. **Look at the attendee list in the [Details] tab**
   Check whether the person is there.
3. **If they're not listed, add them with [Add participant]**
   You can then choose them as a speaker.
4. **Click the speaker name to fix in the [Transcript] tab**
   A menu for choosing the speaker opens.
5. **Look at the scope options at the bottom of the menu**
   They are **[Apply to all of this speaker's lines]** and **[Apply to this line only]**. The default is all lines.
6. **To fix one line, choose [Apply to this line only]**
   Set the scope before you choose a name.
7. **Type a name in [Search by name]**
   Matching attendees appear in a list.
8. **Choose the attendee**
   ==It applies right away with the scope you set.==
9. **Move to the remaining speakers with [Unassigned N]**
   Go to the next speaker and assign it the same way.
10. **To start over, click [Reset all assigned speakers]**
    It's at the very bottom of the **[Speakers]** panel.
