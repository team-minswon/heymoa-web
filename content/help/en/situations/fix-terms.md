## What's happening {#problem}

Product names or English acronyms (like ROAS or CTR) keep showing up as different words in the meeting transcript.

## Where to look first {#where}

Go to **[Settings]** → **[Workspace]** → **[Glossary]**, and to **[Meeting context]** in the note's **[Details]** tab.

## Try these steps {#steps}

::clip{name="dictionary-batch" alt="Video of entering three comma-separated terms in the glossary field and adding them at once" caption="Separate terms with commas to add them at once."}

::clip{name="context-autosave" alt="Video of entering a topic and background in meeting context, which saves automatically" caption="Meeting context saves automatically when you stop typing."}

1. **Open [Glossary] before the meeting starts**
   Go to **[Settings]** → **[Workspace]** → **[Glossary]**.
2. **Enter the terms that get transcribed wrong in [Terms to add]**
   ==Separate multiple terms with commas.==
3. **Check the preview below the field**
   Look for an **[Already added]** or **[Over 40 characters]** label.
4. **Press Enter to add them**
   The terms go into the glossary.
5. **Fill in [Topic] and [Background] under [Meeting context] in the note's [Details] tab**
   It saves automatically when you stop typing.
6. **Set the [Attendees]**
   Attendee names are also used as hints for transcription.
7. **Start the meeting**
   ==Context you change while recording takes effect the next time you continue recording.==
