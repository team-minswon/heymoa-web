## What's happening {#problem}

The summary has a wrong decision or is missing a task. You want to fix it before it goes into the project.

## Where to look first {#where}

The **[Summary]** tab must be open in **[In review]**. If it already shows **[Confirmed]**, you can't edit the review items.

## Try these steps {#steps}

::clip{name="review-confirm" alt="Video of expanding items on the review screen to check their sources, then clicking Finish review to confirm" caption="Expanding items to check their sources, then confirming with [Finish review]."}

1. **Skim the overview and topics in the [Summary] tab**
   Get a sense of what was captured first.
2. **Click a decision or task that looks off**
   The lines it was based on expand.
3. **Click a source line**
   You see what was actually said at that line of the transcript.
4. **Click [Edit] on anything that's wrong**
   An input field opens.
5. **Press Enter to save**
   Your change applies right away.
6. **Click [Exclude] on items you don't want to keep**
   ==Excluded items aren't added to the project.==
7. **Add anything that's missing**
   Click **[Add decision]** or **[Add task]** under the section.
8. **Choose an assignee from [Set assignee] on a task**
   You can set one for each task.
9. **Choose a date from [Set due date]**
   Click a date to set it.
10. **If there are suggestions, choose them one at a time**
    These are **[Replaces an earlier decision]** and **[Change existing task]** suggestions.
11. **Click [Finish review] in the bar at the bottom**
    A confirmation dialog opens.
12. **Click [Finish review] again in the dialog**
    ==This is what adds everything to the project.== Nothing is added before you click it.
