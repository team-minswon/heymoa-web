## What's happening {#problem}

You want to check what was decided in a meeting a few weeks ago, and who said it and why.

## Where to look first {#where}

Use **[My agent]** in the bottom right, and the note's **[Summary]**, **[Timeline]**, and **[Transcript]** tabs.

## Try these steps {#steps}

::clip{name="agent-ask" alt="Video of opening a new chat in My agent, choosing to search meeting notes, asking a question, and watching the answer stream in" caption="Asking My agent about a past meeting."}

1. **Click [My agent] in the bottom right**
   The agent panel opens.
2. **Start a new chat with [+]**
   An empty input field appears.
3. **Type @ in the input field to add a scope**
   ==Adding the project or meeting notes to search makes answers more accurate.==
4. **Ask a specific question**
   Type something like “How did we split the May budget?” and press `kbd:Enter`.
5. **Click [Referenced meeting notes] under the answer**
   See ==which meeting the answer came from==.
6. **In that meeting's [Timeline] tab, click the [Decisions] filter**
   This gives you a closer look.
7. **Click the source timestamp**
   See what was actually said in the transcript.
8. **You can also search by keyword yourself**
   For an ended meeting, use **[Search]** in the **[Transcript]** tab.
