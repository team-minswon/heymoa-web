## What's happening {#problem}

Tasks from the meeting don't have an assignee yet. Teammates want to see only their own tasks.

## Where to look first {#where}

Before the review is confirmed, check tasks in the note's **[Summary]** tab. ==After it's confirmed, check them on the **[Tasks]** screen in the sidebar.==

## Try these steps {#steps}

::clip{name="tasks-filter" alt="Video of switching between the Done and In progress tabs on the Tasks screen and turning on My tasks to filter" caption="Filtering with the tabs and [My tasks]."}

1. **Before confirming, click [Set assignee] on a task in the [Summary] tab**
   Pick a person.
2. **Click [Set due date] on the same row**
   Pick a date.
3. **Confirm with [Finish review]**
   The tasks are added to the project.
4. **Open [Tasks] in the sidebar**
   Confirmed tasks appear in a table.
5. **Click [Set assignee] on a row with no assignee**
   Choose who owns it.
6. **Teammates turn on [My tasks]**
   They see ==only their own tasks== in one place.
7. **Pick a project from [All projects]**
   The list narrows to that project.
8. **Click [Add task] for work that came up outside a meeting**
   Tasks you create yourself go into the same table.
