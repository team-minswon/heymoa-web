## New here? Where do I start? {#problem}

HeyMoa transcribes your meeting as you record. After you end the meeting, AI organizes a summary, decisions, and tasks. Review the results, then choose which items to add to your project.

## Meeting notes template and example {#template}

Copy this template when writing meeting notes yourself. Separate what the team agreed from what needs to happen next, rather than transcribing every remark.

```text
Meeting name:
Date, time, and attendees:
Topics discussed:
Agreed decisions:
Action item / assignee / due date:
Open questions:
What to check next:
```

This fictional example shows how to fill it in.

- **Topic**: Decide the scope of a new welcome screen.
- **Decision**: Show the setup process in three steps on the first screen.
- **Action item**: The designer will prepare a draft before the next meeting.
- **Open question**: Choose where to put the skip button after reviewing the draft.

## What to check before sharing AI meeting notes {#check}

Treat the AI summary as a draft. Check these points against the source remarks.

- Make sure a suggestion hasn't been recorded as a decision everyone agreed to.
- Check that the assignee and due date were actually agreed. Leave them blank and ask the team if they weren't.
- Review open questions and changed decisions. Choose whether an earlier decision should be replaced or kept.

After reviewing, you can [save the meeting notes as a PDF or email them](help:export). For video calls, first check [how to record the other people's voices](help:record-call-audio).

## Where to look first {#where}

Start on the **[All notes]** screen in the sidebar. The **[New note]** button at the top is where it begins.

## Try these steps {#steps}

::clip{name="new-meeting-record" alt="Video of clicking New note, entering a meeting name, creating it, and starting a recording as speech is transcribed" caption="Creating a meeting and starting the recording."}

1. **Click [New note] at the top**
   The **[New meeting]** dialog opens.
2. **Enter a [Meeting name]**
   Today's date is filled in. You can keep it as is.
3. **Check the project**
   However, you can't change the project after the meeting is created.
4. **Click [Create]**
   The note opens.
5. **Choose attendees**
   Pick them from **[Add participant]** in the **[Details]** tab. ==Set them before you end the meeting.==
6. **Click the red dot button**
   It's the **[Start meeting]** button at the bottom center of the note.
7. **Allow microphone access**
   Allow it when your browser asks.
8. **Watch the transcript come in**
   What's said is transcribed in the **[Transcript]** tab.
9. **When the meeting is over, click [Pause]**
   Recording pauses.
10. **Click [End meeting] at the top**
    A confirmation dialog opens.
11. **Click [End meeting] again in the dialog**
    ==This is what ends the meeting.==
12. **Wait for the analysis to finish in the [Summary] tab**
    It's usually ready within 1 minute.
13. **Name any unnamed speakers**
    Click a speaker name and choose who it is.
14. **Check the decisions and tasks**
    Skim them for anything wrong.
15. **Turn on [Add to project] for the items you want to add**
    Choose each decision and task. Items you leave off stay in the meeting notes.
16. **Choose what to do with suggestions on selected items**
    For earlier decisions, choose **[Replace]** or **[Keep]**. For existing task changes, choose **[Apply]** or **[Skip]**.
17. **Click [Finish review], then click it again in the confirmation**
    Only the selected decisions and tasks are added to the project. You can't undo a confirmed review, so check your selections and edits first.
