## View all tasks {#list}

Open **[Tasks]** in the sidebar to see your workspace's tasks. ==When you confirm a meeting review, the tasks you included appear here.==

![The Tasks screen with the In progress tab selected and a table of tasks under the Overdue group](img:tasks/table.webp "The Tasks screen. Filter by status, My tasks, and project at the top.")

::clip{name="tasks-filter" alt="Clicking the Done and In progress tabs and the My tasks filter on the Tasks screen" caption="Filtering with the status tabs and [My tasks]."}

- **[In progress]**, **[Done]**, **[Canceled]** tabs: Each tab shows a count.
- **[My tasks]**: Turn it on to see only tasks assigned to you.
- **[All projects]**: Click it to narrow down by project.

The **[In progress]** tab groups tasks into **[Overdue]**, **[This week]**, **[Next week and later]**, and **[No due date]**. The columns are **[Task]**, **[Project]**, **[Assignee]**, and **[Due date]**.

Tasks load 30 at a time. To see more, click **[Show more tasks]**.

## Edit tasks and see history {#edit}

1. **Mark it done**
   Click the circle on the left of the row. Click it again to reopen the task.
2. **Set the assignee**
   Click **[Set assignee]** and choose a member, a guest participant, or an unnamed speaker.
3. **Set the due date**
   Click **[Set due date]** and pick a date.

In-progress tasks past their due date show in red. A task is in progress, done, or canceled.

Hover over a row to show **[History]** and **[Edit]**. History shows who created and changed the task. Changes from confirmed meetings are listed too, newest first.

Any workspace member can edit a task. If someone else edited it first, you'll see “Someone else edited this first, so the latest version was loaded.” Check the latest version, then make your edit again.

## Add a task yourself {#add}

You can add work that came up outside a meeting as a project task.

1. **Click [Add task]**
   A form opens.
2. **Fill in [Task] and [Project]**
   ==Both fields are required.==
3. **Set [Assignee] and [Due date] if you need to**
   These two fields are optional.

To create tasks while wrapping up a meeting, see [Reviewing and confirming the summary](help:review).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### When do meeting tasks show up here?
When you confirm the meeting review, the tasks you included appear. They don't show up before you confirm.

### Can I see only my tasks?
Yes. Turn on **[My tasks]** to see only tasks assigned to you.

### Can I edit tasks someone else created?
Yes. Any workspace member can edit them.
