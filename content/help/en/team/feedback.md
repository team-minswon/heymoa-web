## Send a request {#send}

1. **Click [Help & feedback] in the sidebar**
   The request window opens.
2. **Pick a category**
   Choose **[Problem or error]**, **[Question]**, **[Feature request]**, or **[Other]**.
3. **Write your message**
   ==You can write up to 1,200 characters.==
4. **Click [Send]**
   Your request is submitted.

:::problem
**Symptom:** “You've sent requests too often. Please try again later.” appears
**Cause:** You went over the number of requests you can send in an hour (5).
**Fix:** Wait a while, then send it again.
:::

## Check for replies {#answers}

Requests you send appear in the **[My requests]** tab as **[Received]**. When the team replies, the status changes to **[Answered]**.

When there's a new reply, a number appears next to **[Help & feedback]** in the sidebar. Opening **[My requests]** marks replies as read.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Is my meeting content sent with a request?
No. Only your current page path and environment are included. Meeting content is never sent.

### Where do I see replies?
In the **[My requests]** tab of **[Help & feedback]**. When there's a new reply, a number appears in the sidebar.

### Can I send as many requests as I want?
No. There's a limit on how many you can send in an hour. If you hit it, wait a while and send again.
