## Sidebar {#sidebar}

Click the workspace name at the top to switch to another workspace or create a **[New workspace]**. You also open **[Workspace settings]** from here.

Below that are **[Notifications]**, **[All notes]**, **[Tasks]**, and your list of projects. **[User guide]** and **[Help & feedback]** sit below the projects. Click your name at the very bottom to find **[Account settings]**, **[Show the intro again]**, and **[Log out]**.

Press `kbd:⌘B` (`kbd:Ctrl+B` on Windows) to collapse or expand the sidebar.

## Creating and deleting projects {#projects}

Meetings are created inside a project. Every new workspace comes with a **[General]** project.

- **Create**: Click **[+]** next to **[Projects]** in the sidebar. Names can be up to 100 characters. Any member can create a project.
- **Rename**: Hover over a project, click the **[⋯]** that appears, and choose **[Rename]**. Any member can rename a project.
- **See a project's notes**: Click a project name to narrow the note list to that project. Click **[All notes]** to see everything again.
- **Delete**: Choose **[Delete]** from **[⋯]**. Only admins can do this.

> [!Caution]
> ==You can't delete a project that has even one note in it.== Delete its notes first, then delete the project.

## Reading the note list {#note-list}

Meetings are grouped by the date they started, newest first. Each row shows:

- **Status**: Not started, Recording, Paused, or Ended.
- **Recording time and number of participants**: In the middle of the row.
- **Participant profile photos**: On the right, up to 4 people.

![Notifications, All notes, Tasks, and the project list in the left sidebar, with meetings grouped by date in the note list on the right](img:workspace-basics/note-list.webp "The sidebar and the note list. Hover over a row to see [⋯] at the right end.")

Click a note row to open it in side view (a panel that shows the note beside the list). From **[⋯]** at the end of the row, you can open it in **[Full screen]**. Admins also see **[Delete]**.

To load older meetings, click **[Show older notes]** at the bottom of the list.

> [!Can't be undone]
> ==Deleting a note permanently removes the meeting's recording, transcript, summary, and evidence.== Decisions and tasks you've already confirmed stay. Agent conversations that asked only about this note are deleted too. Only admins can delete notes. Deleting a meeting that is recording also ends the recording. While a meeting is being analyzed, you can delete it once the analysis finishes.

## Reading a note {#note-view}

A note has **[Details]**, **[Transcript]**, **[Timeline]**, and **[Summary]** tabs. In side view, **[Timeline]** is hidden until the meeting starts, and **[Summary]** appears only after the meeting ends.

Use the full screen icon at the top to switch between side view and full screen. Click **[Back to list]** to return to the note list. The view is saved in the address (`?view=`), so a shared link opens in the same view.

In the **[Details]** tab, you can edit the title. Titles can be up to 200 characters. Click **[Save changes]** when you're done. **[Meeting info]** at the bottom shows the host, total recording time, who can see the note (workspace members), when it was created, and when it was last edited.

> [!Note]
> Any workspace member can view, record, end, and edit a note. Only deleting a note is limited to admins.

## Opening settings {#settings}

- **Workspace settings**: Open **[Workspace settings]** from the workspace name menu at the top of the sidebar.
- **Account settings**: Open **[Account settings]** from the name menu at the bottom.

The left side of the window has two groups.

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **[Workspace]** | **[General]**, **[Members]**, **[Guest participants]**, **[Glossary]**, **[Integrations]**, **[MCP connections]** |
| **[Account]** | **[My account]**, **[Notifications]**, **[My MCP connections]** |

In **[General]**, you edit the workspace name and description. Only admins can do this.

The settings tab you have open is saved in the address (`?settings=`). Press Back to close the settings window, and share the address to open the same settings tab.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Can members create projects too?
Yes, any member can create and rename projects. Only deleting is left to admins.

### I want to delete a project that has notes.
You can't delete a project that has even one note. Delete its notes first, then delete the project. Deleting notes is also for admins.

### What's the difference between side view and full screen?
Side view is the panel that opens on the right when you click a note row. You open full screen from **[Full screen]** in **[⋯]** or with the icon at the top. In side view, the **[Summary]** tab appears only after the meeting ends.
