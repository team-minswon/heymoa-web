## Signing in and out {#sign-in}

You sign in with a Google account only. As long as you keep using HeyMoa, you stay signed in. However, ==if you don't use it for 30 days, you need to sign in again==.

To log out:

1. **Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar**
   A menu opens.
2. **Choose [Log out]**
   This logs you out of the current browser only. Your other devices stay signed in.

Your account name and photo are copied from Google once, when you first sign up. If you change them in Google later, HeyMoa doesn't pick up the change.

## Account settings {#my-account}

Open **[Account settings]** from the name menu at the bottom of the sidebar to see your **[Email]** and **[Account name]**. You can't change either of them here.

The name other people see in the workspace is set separately, in the **[Members]** tab. See [Members and guest participants](help:members#manage) for how.

Turn off the **[Send usage statistics]** switch to stop sending which features you use. Even when it's on, HeyMoa never sends what was said in your meetings.

> [!Note]
> The usage statistics setting applies only to the browser you're using now.

## Deleting your data {#data}

When you delete a note, the meeting's audio, transcript, and summary are deleted with it. To see which meetings can be deleted, read [Screen layout and projects](help:workspace-basics#note-list).

You can't delete your account from inside the app. To see, correct, or delete your personal information, send a request using the method in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

For a short overview of where and how your data is processed, read [Where and how is my data stored?](help:data-privacy).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### If I log out, am I logged out on my other devices too?
No. Only the browser you're using now is logged out. Your other devices stay signed in.

### Can I change my account name?
Not in **[Account settings]**. You can change the name people see in the workspace in the **[Members]** tab instead.

### Can I delete my account from the app?
No. The app has no account deletion feature. Ask for deletion using the method in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
